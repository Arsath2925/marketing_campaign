🎙️ 2 Key Points to Present & Explain: Advanced Analysis
1
Asymmetry in Output vs. Uniformity in Budgeting

Spend skewness is near-zero (-0.06), proving budgets were allocated relatively uniformly across tests. However, Conversions are heavily right-skewed (+1.24), showing that marketing outputs follow a power-law distribution where a small group of campaigns drive outsized impact.

2
Identification of 11 Statistical Outliers via IQR

Applying the 1.5 × IQR threshold isolated 11 upper-bound outliers exceeding 1,610 conversions. Rather than errors, these represent highly successful, viral campaigns that provide clear templates for future budget scaling.

🎙️ 2 Key Points to Present & Explain: Visual Analytics Dashboard
1
The Spend-to-Conversion Disconnect (Scatter & Heatmap Insight)

The correlation between SpendINR and Conversions is virtually flat at r = +0.04. As visual proof on the Scatter Plot, ₹40k+ campaigns frequently yielded fewer than 200 conversions, whereas low-cost campaigns under ₹10k delivered up to 2,400 conversions. Spending more budget without audience-channel calibration resulted in wasted spend.

2
High Intent Dominance (Bar Chart & Audience Distribution)

The Bar Chart illustrates that Affiliate (20,119) and SMS (18,450) captured over 43% of all conversions. Furthermore, Chart 4 proves that Retirees (13.4% CVR) and Working Professionals (13.1% CVR) convert at significantly higher percentages than broad Small Business audiences.

Executive Recommendations & Strategic Roadmap

1. Shift 25% of Underperforming Budget: Reallocate capital away from low-intent Social Media and cold Display Ads into direct-response channels: Affiliate partnerships and SMS campaigns.
2. Target High-Converting Audience Pairs: Scale Search Ads strictly on Working Professionals and Students, while focusing SMS campaigns on Families and Retirees to maintain blended CPA well below ₹30.
